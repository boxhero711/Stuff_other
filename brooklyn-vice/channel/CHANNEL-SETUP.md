# YouTube channel setup

Do these steps by hand in YouTube Studio. Claude Code does not create accounts or publish (CLAUDE.md section 5).

## 1. Create the channel

1. Sign in to the Google account that will own the channel. Use a brand account so more than one person can manage it.
2. YouTube > Create a channel > use a custom name.
3. Name: **Brooklyn Vice Live**
4. Handle: **@BrooklynViceLive**. Backups if taken: @BrooklynViceLiveTV, @BKViceLive, @BrooklynViceLiveHQ. ("Brooklyn Vice" and @BrooklynVice are taken.)
5. Turn on 2-step verification for the account. Verify the channel by phone (this unlocks custom thumbnails and uploads over 15 minutes).
6. Grab the same handle on Instagram, TikTok and X now, even if you post later.

## 2. Branding (Studio > Customization > Branding)

## 4. Layout (Studio > Customization > Layout)

* Channel trailer for people who have not subscribed: the 45-second trailer from `GROWTH-PLAYBOOK.md`.
* Featured video for returning subscribers: the latest Sunday recap.
* Sections in this order: Shorts, Sunday Recap (playlist), One Detail You Missed (playlist), Brooklyn vs Leonida (playlist), Stunt or Fail (playlist).

## 6. Upload defaults (Studio > Settings > Upload defaults)

* Title: leave blank.
* Description footer:

```
Footage: [credit line required by the source]
Music: [license source]

Subscribe for daily GTA 6 Shorts and a Sunday recap.
Unofficial fan channel. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
```

* Tags: `GTA 6, GTA VI, GTA 6 gameplay, Vice City, Leonida, Brooklyn Vice Live`
* Visibility: **Private**. Nothing goes public until the compliance gate (CLAUDE.md section 4) passes and a human approves.
* Category: **Gaming**. Then set the game title field on each video to Grand Theft Auto VI. This links the video to the game's page and search results.
* Made for kids: **No**.
* Altered or synthetic content: answer per video. Real host voice over real gameplay is "No". AI voice or AI faces is "Yes".
* Comments: on, with "Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review".

## 7. Before the first upload

* Studio > Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility: confirm intermediate and advanced features are on.
* Add blocked words for comment spam (crypto, "free money", mod menu links).
* Fill in `compliance/sources.csv` for every source used.
* Link the Instagram account from Instagram (Edit profile > Links).
