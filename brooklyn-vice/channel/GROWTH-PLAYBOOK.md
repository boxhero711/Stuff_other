# Growth playbook

How to grow fast without breaking CLAUDE.md section 2. Copy rules apply to every title, caption and on-screen line: plain short sentences, state the point flat.

## The launch window

GTA VI releases 2026-11-19. Search for "GTA 6" will be at its highest in the first two weeks. That is the window.

Before release (now to Nov 18). No game footage. Nothing from leaks or pre-release.
* Set up the channel and branding (`CHANNEL-SETUP.md`).
* Record the channel trailer with the host on camera and our own Brooklyn footage. No game footage.
* Post 2 or 3 host-only Shorts filmed in Brooklyn: "What a New Yorker expects from Vice City." These build the face and voice before the game drops.
* Sign up 3 to 5 creators for clipping deals (`outreach/`). Ask them to send source files directly.
* Prepare scripts for the first week's Shorts in `scripts/`. Leave the footage slots empty.

Release week (Nov 19 to 25).
* First upload goes out only after the official release time.
* Use our own capture first. Source creators will need a few days to send files.
* One Short per day minimum. A second Short on days with a strong moment, if it passes the "recognizably different" test.
* First Sunday recap on Nov 22 or Nov 29, depending on how much approved footage exists.

## Hooks: the first 2 seconds

Most Shorts are lost in the first 2 seconds. Open on the moment or on the host's face mid-reaction. No intro card at the start of a Short (put `title-9x16.png` at the end).

Hook lines that fit the brand:
* "In Brooklyn this gets you arrested."
* "Nobody in New York would park like this."
* "This detail took me three days to notice."
* "This is the worst stunt I have seen this week."
* "Leonida drivers are worse than New York cabs."

Write each one fresh for its clip. Reusing the same hook every day is what the inauthentic content policy catches.

## Retention

* Cut every pause. Zoom on the key moment. Captions on every line (from the edit pipeline).
* Shorts: 20 to 40 seconds. End on a line that loops back into the start.
* Long-form: 8 to 15 minutes. A new segment or visual change every 30 to 60 seconds. Say what is coming in the first 20 seconds.
* Last 20 seconds of long-form: `end-card.png`, with one video and the subscribe element.

## Titles

Patterns that work for this format:
* `New Yorker reacts to [place or thing] in GTA 6`
* `Brooklyn rules vs Leonida rules: [topic]`
* `[N] GTA 6 details you missed in [area]`
* `The worst GTA 6 fail this week`
* `GTA 6 week [N] recap`

Under 60 characters. "GTA 6" near the front. No story beats or character fates. The title promises what the video shows.

## Thumbnails

* Use `node brand/render.js thumbnail` (see `brand/BRAND.md`).
* 3 to 5 words. One gold word. Host face on the right.
* Test two thumbnails per long-form with YouTube's Test & Compare (Studio, on the video's details page).

## Collabs and outreach

The creators we clip are also our best growth channel.
* Every clip credits and links the creator, which gives them a reason to share it.
* Offer a monthly "creator spotlight" Short on our channel for creators who sign up.
* Reply to every comment in the first hour after an upload. Pin a question to start a thread.

## Community

* Community post every 2 to 3 days: polls such as "Brooklyn or Leonida: where is parking worse?"
* Use comments to pick topics for "One detail you missed" and credit the commenter on screen.

## Numbers to watch (Studio > Analytics)

| Metric | Target to start | What to do if it misses |
|---|---|---|
| Shorts viewed vs swiped away | Over 70% viewed | Rewrite the first 2 seconds |
| Long-form click-through rate | 5% or more | Test new thumbnails and titles |
| Long-form average view duration | 40% or more | Cut the intro, add segment changes |
| Returning viewers | Rising week over week | Lean on the series that bring them back |

## Never do

* Buy views, subscribers, or engagement. Sub4sub. These break YouTube policy and can get the channel removed.
* Promise money glitches, cheats, mod menus, or free GTA in titles or thumbnails.
* Use leaked or pre-release footage.
* Boost a video with game footage as a paid post or ad (CLAUDE.md 2.1).
* Repost another creator's video without an approved route (CLAUDE.md 2.6).

## Channel trailer script (45 seconds, host on camera in Brooklyn)

```
[0-3s, host walking past a Brooklyn street sign]
I'm from Brooklyn. I've been waiting thirteen years for this game.

[3-12s, host at the waterfront, bridge behind]
GTA 6 is set in Vice City. I'm going to play it like a New Yorker.
I'll check whether the rules here work there.

[12-30s, quick cuts of the host talking, title card, graffiti taglines]
Every day there's a Short. A detail you missed. A stunt. A fail.
Every Sunday there's a recap of the whole week.
No story spoilers. Ever.

[30-45s, host to camera, end on logo]
Same hustle. New city.
Subscribe to Brooklyn Vice.
```

No game footage in the trailer, so it can go up before release.
